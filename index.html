ISO 42001 & ISO 27001 Conceptual Breakdown Standard Specifications

ISO 42001 (AI Management System)

Definition & Meaning: The international standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS). It specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system within organizations. Focuses on responsible AI development, ethical governance, risk mitigation, and transparency.

ISO 27001 (Information Security)

Definition & Meaning: The premier global standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). It provides requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving information security controls to safeguard sensitive client and organizational data against emerging threats.
